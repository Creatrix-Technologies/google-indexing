FAQ

Questions about plans

Is this allowed for my pages?

Google officially supports the Indexing API for pages with JobPosting or BroadcastEvent structured data. For other pages a submission asks Google to crawl sooner but cannot guarantee indexing, and Google screens all submissions for spam. You use your own Google project, so its 200 submissions a day are yours, and we never work around that quota. The crawl, the URL Inspection status and the explanations of why a page is not indexed work for every page regardless.

What platforms are supported?

Any website with publicly accessible URLs: WordPress, WooCommerce, Shopify, nopCommerce, Next.js, or fully custom-built sites.

Does it work for existing pages?

Yes. You can submit both new pages and updated existing pages, so Google sees your latest content.

Is this affiliated with Google?

No. GoogleIndexing.com is an independent service that automates Google's publicly documented Indexing API. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by Google LLC. Google is a trademark of Google LLC.
